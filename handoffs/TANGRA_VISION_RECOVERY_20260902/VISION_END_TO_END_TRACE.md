## Known historical coordinate and time hazards

Historical documentation identified:

- Detector/NanoTracker/CA operated in a 640×640 pixel coordinate model.
- Runtime camera projection FOV and nominal HQ manufacturer-spec FOV were inconsistent.
- CA future pixel and projection/range were not wired into one synchronized future metric chain.
- Detection, projection and world-map timestamps had different owners.
- Range enrichment had duplicate historical locations.
- Camera-to-carrier extrinsics and carrier attitude were absent from the audited metric chain.

These are metric-chain limitations, not evidence that the validated HQ detector/tracker should be redesigned.
