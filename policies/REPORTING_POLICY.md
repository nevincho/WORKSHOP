# REPORTING POLICY

STATUS: MANDATORY
APPLIES TO: WORKSHOP CONTROLLER AND ALL PROJECT REPORTS

## Cadence
- 8 hours: operational report
- 12 hours: intermediate engineering report
- 24 hours: full engineering report

Reports do not pause autonomous work unless a human gate exists.

## Required report fields
- reporting window;
- projects processed;
- tasks completed and independently validated;
- tasks reworked or failed;
- blockers and root causes;
- current validated checkpoints/rollback references;
- Codex usage and justification;
- protected-component status;
- tests actually run and what objective they measured;
- NOT VERIFIED items;
- next eligible tasks;
- human decisions required, if any.

## Evidence rule
A report is a summary, not proof. Claims of completion/validation must point to repository/runtime evidence. Agent reports alone do not establish project state.

## Notification rule
Do not notify Vlad between scheduled reporting windows unless a human gate requires intervention.
